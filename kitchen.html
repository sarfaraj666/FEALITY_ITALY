BEAUTY MEETS RESILIENCE

The thinking behind steel.

Steel is more than an industrial material. When selected and manufactured correctly, it can bring modern living a practical blend of performance and design.

✦

Moisture-aware design

Well-specified steel cabinetry does not swell from absorbing moisture like many wood-based boards.

✦

Easy-to-clean surfaces

Appropriate non-porous finishes can be wiped down with everyday, suitable cleaning methods.

✦

Built around daily use

Material grade, fabrication and finishing can support a robust kitchen over time.

A MATERIAL POINT OF VIEW

Beyond what meets the eye.

Choosing the right materials shapes how an interior feels and performs. Steel can resist termite damage and moisture-related board swelling, while finishes, fabrication and care remain important.

UNDERSTANDING THE MATERIAL

Good design begins with good questions.

Helpful answers about steel-led interiors and sensible material choices.

Is steel a good choice for kitchen cabinetry? +

Steel can be a strong, practical choice, particularly in moisture-prone kitchens. Performance depends on grade, finishing, construction and installation.

Does steel rust? +

Some steel grades can corrode when improperly specified or maintained. Confirm material grades and suitable finishes with the manufacturer.

Does a steel kitchen need special cleaning? +

Generally, mild soap, a soft cloth and drying are appropriate. Avoid harsh abrasives and follow finish-specific care guidance.

Can steel cabinetry have warm, luxurious finishes? +

Yes. Steel-based construction can work with many design directions, including stone-inspired, matte and metallic looks, depending on the real product options.

Is steel always better than wood? +

Not always. Steel offers certain advantages in moisture and termite resistance; wood and engineered boards can offer different aesthetic and cost benefits.
