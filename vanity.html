BEAUTY MEETS RESILIENCE

The thinking behind steel.

Steel is more than an industrial material. When selected and manufactured correctly, it can bring modern living a practical blend of performance and design.

✦

Moisture-conscious choices

Properly specified steel cabinetry can be well suited to humid interiors when corrosion protection is appropriate.

✦

Straightforward upkeep

Suitable metal finishes can be easy to maintain with gentle, regular cleaning.

✦

Design flexibility

UNDERSTANDING THE MATERIAL

Good design begins with good questions.

Helpful answers about steel-led interiors and sensible material choices.

Is steel suitable for bathrooms? +

The right grade and protective finish can suit bathroom environments. Selection depends on humidity, exposure and maintenance.

Will the finish corrode? +

Corrosion resistance varies by grade and finishing, so product specifications must be confirmed.

Can vanity units have drawers and mirrors? +

Many modern vanity concepts can integrate drawers, lighting and mirrors; available FEALITY configurations need confirmation.

Can a small vanity still look luxurious? +

Yes. Proportion, lighting, finish and smart storage often matter more than size.

How do I maintain a steel vanity? +

Wipe with a suitable mild cleaner, dry damp surfaces where appropriate and avoid abrasives.
