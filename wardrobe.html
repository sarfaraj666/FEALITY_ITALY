BEAUTY MEETS RESILIENCE

The thinking behind steel.

Steel is more than an industrial material. When selected and manufactured correctly, it can bring modern living a practical blend of performance and design.

✦

Termite resistance

Steel itself is not a food source for termites, unlike wood and many wood-based materials.

✦

Stable cabinetry

Properly constructed steel panels avoid moisture-related swelling associated with many wood-based boards.

✦

Finishes for modern homes

Metal-based systems can complement glass, mirror and a range of decorative surface treatments.

A MATERIAL POINT OF VIEW

Beyond what meets the eye.

Choosing the right materials shapes how an interior feels and performs. Steel can resist termite damage and moisture-related board swelling, while finishes, fabrication and care remain important.

UNDERSTANDING THE MATERIAL

Good design begins with good questions.

Helpful answers about steel-led interiors and sensible material choices.

Can wardrobes be made from steel? +

Yes. Metal-based cabinetry can support modern wardrobe designs, with hardware and finish selected for the intended use.

Will a steel wardrobe look industrial? +

Not necessarily. Contemporary surface finishes can create a soft, elegant and residential aesthetic.

Is steel termite-resistant? +

Steel is not eaten by termites, although any other materials used with the system must be considered separately.

Can I customize the wardrobe? +

Different configurations can be explored; final customisation options must be confirmed with the business.

Do steel wardrobes require care? +

Use the cleaning method advised for the finish and keep sliding or hinged hardware maintained as directed.
