THE FEALITY PHILOSOPHY

The beauty of better materials.

We believe the best interiors bring beauty and everyday practicality into the same conversation. FEALITY ITALY is presented here with a steel-first design philosophy for kitchens, wardrobes and vanities.

Steel can provide distinct benefits in suitable applications—from resisting termite damage to avoiding moisture-driven swelling found in some wood-based boards. Correct grade, finishing and expert installation remain central to long-term performance.

Steel is a compelling interior option when specified for the environment. It deserves a balanced conversation based on genuine material properties.

✦

Built for living

MATERIAL EDUCATION

Steel & wood. Know the difference.

Every material has strengths. The right choice depends on the room, design, budget, grade and construction.

CONSIDERATION STEEL CABINETRY WOOD-BASED CABINETRY
Moisture Steel panels don't absorb water and swell Some boards can swell if exposed
Termites Steel itself isn't eaten by termites Wood and some boards may need protection
Maintenance Depends on finish and corrosion protection Depends on sealing, finish and humidity
Appearance Minimal, metallic and coated looks Natural grains and many decorative textures

A BETTER UNDERSTANDING

Material questions, answered.

A straightforward introduction to steel and its practical use in homes.

What makes steel useful in an interior? +

Appropriately selected steel can provide practical resistance to termites and moisture-related swelling while supporting clean-lined design.

Is all steel corrosion-resistant? +

No. Corrosion resistance varies with steel grade, environment, finishing and maintenance.

Is steel always better than wood? +

Not necessarily. Both materials have benefits and trade-offs. FEALITY can explain the actual specifications of its available products.
